Back-Safe Strength Circuit

35 minutes. Dumbbells + one resistance band. Every move below is chosen to keep your lumbar spine neutral — no flexion-under-load, no heavy axial compression.

Confirmed from your MRI (08/12/2025): L4-L5 has a posterior central annular tear with mild disc extrusion and right-sided nerve root abutment. L5-S1 has a left paracentral annular fissure with left-sided abutment. Whole-spine screening also shows loss of cervical lordosis with disc dehydration at every cervical level (no cervical bulge). This is more specific than "disc bulge" — an annular tear and extrusion mean the disc's outer ring is compromised, so the flexion/rotation/compression rules below aren't precautionary, they're load-bearing. This is not a diagnosis or treatment plan — it's a lay reading of the radiologist's report to shape exercise selection. Your physio/ortho's restrictions override anything here.
Rule this circuit follows: no sit-ups/crunches, no loaded spinal flexion or twisting, no standing overhead work without support, no heavy axial loading (squats/deadlifts done heavy), neutral neck throughout (given the cervical findings — don't crank your head up or let it hang in any move below). Every "avoid" note below tells you exactly what mistake would break that rule.
Stop-rule: with confirmed nerve root abutment on both sides (right at L4-L5, left at L5-S1), any tingling, numbness, or shooting pain into either leg during a move means stop that exercise for the day — don't push through it hoping it clears up. Muscle fatigue and burn are fine; nerve-type pain is not.
